IaaS (Infrastructure as a Service)

IaaS stands for Infrastructure as a Service in cloud computing. It allows companies to rent fundamental computing resources over the internet. These resources include virtual servers, storage space, routers, and physical networking hardware. You do not need to purchase or maintain physical hardware inside an office building. Users maintain full control over their operating systems, installed applications, and network settings. The cloud provider only manages the underlying hardware, physical facilities, and power systems. Overall, IaaS offers high flexibility and saves money on hardware investments.
