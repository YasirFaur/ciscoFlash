IPS (Intrusion Prevention System)

An Intrusion Prevention System, or IPS, is an advanced network security technology. It acts like an intelligent guard that inspects all traffic passing through your network. Unlike a basic firewall, an IPS inspects data deeply at the application layer. It analyzes network packets in real time to detect malicious behavior and cyber threats. When it finds an attack, it blocks the bad traffic immediately to protect your servers. It uses signature-based detection and behavioral analysis to stop known and unknown attacks. Deploying an IPS ensures your organization stays safe from modern digital threats.
