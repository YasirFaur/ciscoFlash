CCC (Cisco Catalyst Center)

Cisco Catalyst Center is a centralized management platform for modern networks. It acts as the brain of your network infrastructure. It uses intent-based networking to convert simple business goals into precise device configurations. It automates daily tasks like device setups, updates, and troubleshooting. Administrators can also create security rules to separate different groups of users. Real-time analytics help monitor network health and detect potential errors. Overall, it makes large network management faster, safer, and easier.
