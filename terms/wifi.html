Wi-Fi (Wi-Fi)

Wi-Fi is a popular technology that connects your devices to the internet without cables. It uses invisible radio waves to send data through the air between a device and a router. You can easily use it on phones, laptops, and smart TVs at home or in public places. Newer standards like Wi-Fi 6 offer faster speeds and less delay for modern users. Thick walls and home appliances can sometimes weaken the wireless signal. Overall, Wi-Fi makes everyday internet access simple, fast, and continuous.

Configuration Example (Cisco IOS)


! Create the wireless SSID
dot11 ssid Corporate-WiFi
   vlan 10
   authentication open
   authentication key-management wpa2
   wpa-psk ascii 0 MySecurePass123!

! Configure the radio interface
interface Dot11Radio0
   no shutdown
   encryption vlan 10 mode ciphers aes-ccmp
   ssid Corporate-WiFi

! Map wireless traffic to VLAN 10
interface Dot11Radio0.10
   encapsulation dot1Q 10
   bridge-group 1
